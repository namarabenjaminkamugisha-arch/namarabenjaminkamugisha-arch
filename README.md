### 🔐 Hack Reddi — free, offline cybersecurity training platform
**▶️ Live (no install, no sign-up): https://hack-reddi.namarabenjaminkamugisha.workers.dev**

A hands-on, browser-based platform designed to take a complete beginner toward
job-ready across offensive **and** defensive security. It runs entirely
client-side and works offline.

- **Capture-the-flag** challenges with guided, step-by-step walkthroughs
- A **Tools library**, a SoloLearn-style **Code Dojo**, per-topic **exams**, and
  spaced-repetition review
- Installable (PWA) on desktop & mobile, plus a native **desktop app** with
  isolated local labs

`React` · `TypeScript` · `Vite` · `Tailwind CSS` · `WebAssembly` · `PWA` ·
`Electron` · deployed on `Cloudflare`
