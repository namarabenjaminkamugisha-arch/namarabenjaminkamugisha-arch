## 🔐 Hack Reddi — a free, offline cybersecurity training platform

### ▶️ Try it live (no install, no sign-up): **https://hack-reddi.namarabenjaminkamugisha.workers.dev**

A hands-on, browser-based platform designed to take a complete beginner toward
job-ready across offensive **and** defensive security. Everything runs
client-side and works offline — your data stays in your own browser.

**What's inside**
- Career paths (SOC Analyst, Penetration Tester, AppSec, Blue Team and more),
  structured as foundations → phases → specializations
- A real in-browser **terminal**, a **packet grid**, and live **Python / C / SQL /
  Linux** engines compiled to WebAssembly
- **Capture-the-flag** challenges with guided, step-by-step walkthroughs
- A **Tools library** (how & when to use nmap, Wireshark, Burp, Metasploit, …), a
  SoloLearn-style **Code Dojo**, per-topic **exams**, and spaced-repetition review
- A free **Library** of books, docs and videos, a live **security news** feed, and
  an exportable **portfolio**
- Installable as an app (PWA) on desktop and mobile — plus a native **desktop
  build** for real, isolated local labs

**Built with**

`React` · `TypeScript` · `Vite` · `Tailwind CSS` · `Dexie (IndexedDB)` ·
`Pyodide` / `sql.js` / `JSCPP` / `v86` (WebAssembly) · `PWA` (installable, offline)
· `Electron` (desktop) · deployed on `Cloudflare`
